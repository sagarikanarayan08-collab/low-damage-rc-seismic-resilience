# Methodology

## 1. Building Model

A representative six-storey reinforced-concrete moment-resisting frame is considered for the preliminary study.

## 2. Structural Analysis

The structural model will be evaluated using modal and nonlinear seismic analysis.

The primary response quantities are:

- Fundamental period
- Storey displacement
- Inter-storey drift
- Base shear
- Floor acceleration
- Residual drift
- Energy dissipation

## 3. Earthquake Demand

A suite of earthquake ground motions will be considered to evaluate structural response across different seismic intensity levels.

## 4. Non-Structural Elements

Selected drift-sensitive components will be investigated:

- Partition walls
- Glazing
- Suspended ceilings

## 5. Damage Assessment

Structural and non-structural damage will be classified into multiple damage states based on seismic demand.

## 6. Resilience Assessment

The study will subsequently examine:

- Repair requirements
- Expected downtime
- Residual deformation
- Post-earthquake functionality

## 7. Future Work

The preliminary model will be extended to nonlinear time-history analysis, probabilistic fragility assessment and comparison between conventional and low-damage structural configurations.
